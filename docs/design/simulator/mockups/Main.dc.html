Foundations Internal · /foundations

The electric neon language of Studio: tokens, status, device identity, data lines, controls, motion and wide-screen rules every page reuses. Neon is the style of the whole product (an IoT console), kept legible by four rules: the glow sits outside borders and never behind text, only numerals of 24 px and up may glow, every animation stops under reduced motion, and a screen carries at most 24 glowing lines and 12 pulsing nodes. Not the landing page: Studio opens on Overview. Token names equal the command center’s; the values are Studio’s own and lead its later refresh.

Electric tones: saturated hues that stay readable as text (4.6:1 or more on surface-2) and glow without turning white. Electric cyan #47D7ff (--flow) is energy moving and belongs to data lines and focus; indigo accent means “you can act on this” or “this is selected”, never a fault; neon green is healthy or delivered; amber is anomaly; one hot red serves critical alerts and failed components, told apart by icon and label. Alert severity high keeps an orange (#FF8F3A) of its own so it is not mistaken for amber.

Status language

The edge light is the state, in motion: healthy breathes (3.2 s), an anomaly pulses faster (1.3 s) so it is the first thing the eye finds, down stutters like a failing arc and its dashes march, paused and silent stay dark. A state is still always border style, icon and a word, never colour or light alone. At most 12 nodes animate at once; the rest hold their glow still.

Controls light up when they can act: the primary action and the selected option carry an indigo edge light, hover and focus switch the edge to cyan, danger is red, and a disabled control has no light at all. Every control that acts shows cursor: pointer and answers a hover within 120 ms; a pressed control drops 1 px and dims its glow; a disabled one shows not-allowed and never glows; text fields keep the text cursor. Sliders and progress fill from indigo to cyan like a charging line.

Motion follows the engine’s state, never a loop of its own: a node breathes only while it is in that state. With prefers-reduced-motion the dots, sparks, breathing and flicker are not drawn; the glow stays, still. Caps: 4 dots per line, 24 on screen, 24 glowing lines, 12 animated nodes.

Three kinds, three shapes, one glyph each, so a device is recognisable in a graph, on a card and in 3D without reading its name. The shape is the kind and the edge light is the state (healthy, anomaly, down, left to right). A short code (DC·1 LIB HUB) comes from the zone code and an index. Colour and light are never identity; they are status.

Data lines

Light means energy is moving. A line that carries nothing is dark, so the glow can be trusted. Data lines are where the electric style began and stay the brightest thing on a page. The same three layers (halo, glow, core) now also edge nodes, controls and charts, at lower strength, so a line still reads as the one carrying energy. Capped at 24 glowing lines on screen; reduced motion keeps the glow and stops the pulse.
